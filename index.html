Current work

AIFS-COMPO

AIFS-COMPO extends ECMWF's Artificial Intelligence Forecasting System (AIFS) to atmospheric composition. A single transformer model forecasts meteorological variables together with aerosols (dust, sea salt, biomass-burning aerosol and sulphate), particulate matter (PM2.5 and PM10) and reactive gases such as ozone, nitrogen dioxide, carbon monoxide and sulphur dioxide. It is implemented in Anemoi, the open-source framework for data-driven weather models developed by ECMWF and European meteorological services.

Training

  1. Pre-training on the CAMS reanalysis (EAC4), 2003–2023.
  2. Fine-tuning on operational CAMS analyses, the data the model is initialised from.
  3. Rollout training over multi-step forecasts, for stability at longer lead times.

Evaluation

Compared with the operational IFS-COMPO system against independent observations (Harder et al., 2026):

  • Lower errors for aerosol optical depth at AERONET sites over the five-day range.
  • Comparable or better skill for particulate matter and surface ozone in most regions.
  • Weaker skill for some total-column quantities, such as total column ozone.
  • Ten-day forecasts capture the evolution of the 2024 Antarctic ozone hole.

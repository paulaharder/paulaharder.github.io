Flagship project

AIFS-COMPO

A data-driven forecasting system for atmospheric composition. It extends ECMWF's AI forecasting system (AIFS) from weather to the air itself: one transformer predicts weather together with dust, sea salt, smoke and sulphate aerosols, PM2.5 and PM10, ozone, nitrogen dioxide, carbon monoxide and sulphur dioxide.

187variables forecast together
10 dayslead time, twice the operational CAMS horizon
3 htime steps, so daily pollution peaks show up
~50 sfor a 5-day global forecast on one GPU, against ~1000 s on 8000 CPUs for the physics-based model

How it learns

  1. 1
    Pre-training

    21 years of the CAMS reanalysis (EAC4, 2003–2023) teach the model how weather and chemistry evolve together.

  2. 2
    Fine-tuning

    Operational CAMS analyses bring it to the state the model actually starts from every day.

  3. 3
    Rollout training

    Training on multi-day forecasts keeps it stable and accurate far into the forecast.

What it does well

  • Lower errors than the operational physics-based system, IFS-COMPO, for aerosol optical depth at AERONET stations over five days.
  • Lower PM2.5 and PM10 errors in four of six regional comparisons, with the largest gains over China.
  • Ten-day forecasts that still capture the 2024 Antarctic ozone hole.

What comes next

  • Forecasting wildfire emissions instead of holding them fixed.
  • Running emission scenarios.
  • Learning directly from observations of the atmosphere's composition.

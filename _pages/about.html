Now

I'm a machine learning scientist at ECMWF, the European Centre for Medium-Range Weather Forecasts, where I work for the Copernicus Atmosphere Monitoring Service (CAMS). I build AIFS-COMPO, a data-driven system that forecasts aerosols, reactive gases and air quality for the whole globe. The work runs from training data and model design to evaluation against real-world observations, and it is built on Anemoi, the open-source framework for AI weather models.

Before

  • Mila, MontréalPostdoc with David Rolnick on generative models for downscaling weather and climate data.
  • PhDMachine learning and computer science at Fraunhofer ITWM and the University of Kaiserslautern, together with the University of Oxford. I used deep learning to speed up climate models, supervised by Janis Keuper, Nicolas Gauger and Philip Stier.
  • InternshipsNASA/ESA Frontier Development Lab, Mila, and the Allen Institute for AI.
  • IndustryDeveloper and data scientist in the automotive industry.
  • TübingenMaster's degree in mathematics, specialising in numerical analysis with Christian Lubich.

What I care about

Machine learning that holds up in practice: models that respect physics, keep working when conditions change, and get extreme events right, because those are the days when a forecast matters most.

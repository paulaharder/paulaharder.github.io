Now

I am a machine learning scientist at ECMWF, the European Centre for Medium-Range Weather Forecasts, working on atmospheric composition for the Copernicus Atmosphere Monitoring Service (CAMS). I work on AIFS-COMPO, a data-driven system for forecasting aerosols, reactive gases and air quality, which is developed with many colleagues at ECMWF. My work covers training data, model design and evaluation against observations, within the open-source Anemoi framework.

Before

  • Mila, MontréalPostdoc with David Rolnick on generative models for downscaling weather and climate data.
  • PhDMachine learning and computer science at Fraunhofer ITWM and the University of Kaiserslautern, together with the University of Oxford. I used deep learning to speed up climate models, supervised by Janis Keuper, Nicolas Gauger and Philip Stier.
  • InternshipsNASA/ESA Frontier Development Lab, Mila, and the Allen Institute for AI.
  • IndustryDeveloper and data scientist in the automotive industry.
  • TübingenMaster's degree in mathematics, specialising in numerical analysis with Christian Lubich.

Research interests

Machine learning for environmental, weather and climate modelling, in particular physical consistency, generalisation under distribution shift, and the representation of extreme events.
